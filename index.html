What is Heinis

A pub with a backbone

Ask anyone in Kallio: “Heinis” is where the jukebox is loud, the taps are interesting and nobody cares who you are or where you came from. Rotating craft beers — some brewed in small batches just for us — and a calendar full of bands and DJ nights.
